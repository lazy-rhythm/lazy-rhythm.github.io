Third-party services used by the Application may include:

- Supabase, for anonymous authentication, database services, realtime communication, presence, and room synchronization
- Google AdMob, for displaying advertisements

These third-party providers may process information necessary to provide their services.

Depending on the service, this information may include:

- Anonymous user identifiers
